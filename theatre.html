The Rimalower Prize

the rimalower prize, (named for ben rimalower, the founder of barestage productions), is an annual grant for uc berkeley student theatre productions that uniquely uplift underrepresented voices in the performing arts. it is designed for stories so personal that they become universal.

i created the prize in 2025 after reading madhuri shekar's A Nice Indian Boy and sanaz toosi's English, because the berkeley community deserves to have an home for art that truly represents us. after running a financial audit on barestage productions, i discovered systematic lack of support for productions with diverse themes (i.e., shows that center non-white or lgbtq+ characters). the rimalower prize aims to fill that gap.

this project has now grown to include more performers, artists, technicians, and administrators than i ever could have foreseen—not to mention the countless audience members that have been a part of this journey as well.

JUMP, by charly evon simpson, dir. kalyan piovesan toussaint, was the inaugural awardee. it tells the story of a Black family navigating grief, and strangers finding unlikely connection. i was proud to serve as its producer.

our intrepid cast and crew hurtled through every challenge a new production could throw at us, from securing rehearsal space, to aligning schedules with a wider company, and even to building a freestanding replica of the golden gate bridge.

through JUMP, we fought to create the kind of community we had so hoped to find--and succeeded.

you can read more about the prize on the barestage website, or here, in my original proposal.
