The Rimalower Prize

the rimalower prize is an annual grant i created for uc berkeley student theatre productions that uniquely uplift underrepresented voices in the performing arts. it is named for ben rimalower and hosted through barestage productions.

its purpose is to tell stories so personal that they become universal.

after i read madhuri shekar's A Nice Indian Boy and sanaz toosi's English in 2025, i was struck. where was the place on campus for theatre like this, where i could see myself more than acting on stage?

as financial director at barestage productions, i ran a financial audit uncovering systematic lack of support for productions with diverse themes (i.e., shows that center non-white or lgbtq+ characters). the rimalower prize aims to fill that gap.

since its launch in 2026, the project has grown to include more performers, artists, technicians, and administrators than i ever could have foreseen—not to mention the countless audience members that have been a part of this journey as well.

JUMP, by charly evon simpson, dir. kalyan piovesan toussaint, was the inaugural awardee. it tells the story of a Black family navigating grief, and strangers finding unlikely connection. i was proud to serve as its producer.

we hunted down rehearsal space, scrapped for resources and funding within a wider company, and even built a freestanding replica of the golden gate bridge within a week. through JUMP, we fought to create the kind of community we had so hoped to find--and succeeded.

you can read more about the rimalower prize on the barestage website, or here, in my original proposal.
