Hi! Thanks for stopping by. I'm Jay, an interdisciplinary policy analyst based in sunny Southern California. I'm interested in national security, the Indo-Pacific, and emerging technologies. Yes, that includes AI and machine learning. How could it not? I also cover defense accountability, nuclear policy, and GovTech partnerships.

I recently graduated from UC Berkeley with degrees in Statistics and Political Economy, a concentration in Indo-Pacific Trade and Security, and a minor in Public Policy. I've spent time at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and the Institute for Research on Labor and Employment. I was an Air Force ROTC cadet for a hot minute, and I have about 20-odd flight hours to my name. I really like planes.

I'm also a former theater kid! I'm proudest of the Rimalower Prize, an annual arts grant I started at Berkeley to support a student production that uniquely uplifts underrepresented voices in the performing arts. I've been a performer, writer, director, and dramaturg, but producing the Prize has been the most rewarding. You can read more about JUMP and subsequently awarded prizes here

What else? I speak a few languages. I like experimental recipes and short walks on the beach. I like to teach, and I like to learn. But before anything else, I consider myself a storyteller.
