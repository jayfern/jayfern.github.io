about

at pho 2000 in san francisco, may 2026.

hi! i'm jay, an interdisciplinary policy analyst from sunny southern california. i'm interested in national security, public policy, and regulating emerging technologies. in particular, i'm curious about how advances in ai and machine learning affect defense accountability, lethal autonomous weapons, and govtech partnerships.

i graduated from uc berkeley in 2026 with dual B.A.s in statistics and political economy, and a minor in public policy. i worked on a few cool projects for lawrence livermore national laboratory and the u.s. space force, but spent most of my time in student theatre keeping the stage lights on.

i was also an air force rotc cadet before separating under executive order 14183. the drive to serve never really left my blood. after graduating, i worked on a local campaign for california state assembly, drafting press releases, canvassing, and generally losing sleep over flipping the seat blue.

we did, by the way.

i've been really into experimental recipes, lamenting the fall of the em-dash to ai, and binge-watching prestige tv when i should be writing. i speak a few languages, and teach 3rd graders creative approaches to math in my spare time. shoot me an email or linkedin dm if you'd like to connect, or if you've potentially discovered the next great american novel. i'm always looking for something new to read.
