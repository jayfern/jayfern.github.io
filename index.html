Hi! I'm Jay, an interdisciplinary policy analyst based in sunny Southern California. I'm interested in national security, the Indo-Pacific, and emerging technologies. In particular, I'm curious about how advances in AI and machine learning affect defense accountability, nuclear policy, and GovTech partnerships.

I graduated from UC Berkeley with degrees in Statistics and Political Economy, a concentration in Indo-Pacific Trade and Security, and a minor in Public Policy. I've spent time at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and the Institute for Research on Labor and Employment, working on nuclear safety standards and South Asian electoral politics (respectively). I was also an Air Force ROTC cadet for a hot minute, and logged around 20 flight hours along the way. My most recent project (through Cal's inaugural National Security Resilience Accelerator) was sponsored by SpaceWERX and NASA to create a machine learning product designed to streamline space launch payload logistics. That's my baby, DIANA.

Outside of academia, I'm proudest of the Rimalower Prize, an annual arts grant I created at Berkeley to support a student production that uplifts underrepresented voices in the performing arts. I've been a performer, writer, director, and dramaturg, but producing for JUMP, the Prize's first awardee, has been my most rewarding work. You can read more about JUMP and subsequent recipients here.

What else? I speak a few languages. I like experimental recipes and short walks on the beach. I love my car, Devi. I like to teach, and I really, really like to learn.
