I split my attention between two rooms that don't usually talk to each other: the briefing room and the greenroom. During the day I'm thinking about Indo-Pacific trade and security policy, usually with a dataset open next to a stack of primary sources. At night I'm often backstage, either performing or trying to get a scene I've written to hold together in ninety seconds.

I graduated from UC Berkeley with degrees in Statistics and Political Economy, a concentration in Indo-Pacific Trade and Security, and a minor in Public Policy. I've spent time at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and Berkeley's Institute for Research on Labor and Employment, and served as Cadet Wing Commander in Air Force ROTC.

This site is where those two halves live side by side.
